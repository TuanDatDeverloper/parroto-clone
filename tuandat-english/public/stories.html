Why learn English with stories?

Stories give words a home. When you meet "spin", "promise" or "greedy" inside a tale you care about, the meaning sticks far better than it does on a flashcard. Our stories are classic fairy tales and fables retold in graded English, so every sentence is pitched at your level - simple enough to follow, rich enough to teach you something new.

Each story comes with natural audio narration and word-by-word highlighting, so you can read and listen at the same time. Tap any word to see its meaning and pronunciation, save it to your notebook, and finish with the vocabulary list and grammar patterns the story was built around.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your level

    Filter stories from A1 to C2. The same tale is often retold at several levels, so you can grow with it.

  2. 2

    Listen and read along

    Press play and follow the highlighted words. Tap a sentence to replay it, slow the narration down, or switch to read-only mode.

  3. 3

    Keep what you learned

    Save new words to your notebook, review the grammar patterns, and come back to your saved stories any time.
