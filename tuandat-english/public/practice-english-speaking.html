Practice English Speaking Online with Real People

Free voice chat with real people — practice everyday conversation, improve IELTS & TOEIC speaking skills, and build real-world English fluency

The biggest problem for English learners isn't a lack of materials - it's a lack of a place to actually speak. You study vocabulary, grammar and listening, but without anyone to hold a real conversation with, speaking reflexes never form.

TuanDat solves this directly with free 1-on-1 speaking practice (voice chat) with real people. You're matched with another learner at your level (A1-C2) to talk through topics - from everyday conversation to IELTS/TOEIC Speaking practice. No more studying alone: you speak, you're heard, and natural conversation reflexes build with every chat.

How does speaking practice with real people work?

  1. 1

    Pick a topic & level

    Choose a topic you want to talk about (everyday conversation, travel, work, IELTS Speaking…) and get matched with someone at your CEFR level.

  2. 2

    Join a voice room

    Join a 1-on-1 or small-group room over a voice call. Create a public or private room.

  3. 3

    Talk & build reflexes

    Speak about your chosen topic with ready-made question prompts if you get stuck. The more you talk, the faster your reflexes.

  4. 4

    Build a habit

    Keep your streak and join the leaderboard and community to practice speaking every day.

Why is speaking with real people more effective?

  • Build real reflexes

  • Matched to your level

    Speaking with someone at your level (A1-C2) keeps you from feeling overwhelmed or bored, so you speak up with more confidence.

  • Topics that fit your goal

    Rooms for IELTS Speaking, TOEIC Speaking, Business English, everyday conversation, travel and more - matched to your goal.

  • Free, with people always online

    A large learner community, fast matching, free to use.

Frequently asked questions about practicing English speaking online

Is practicing English speaking with real people on TuanDat free?

Yes. The voice chat speaking-practice matching is free to use - you're paired with other learners to talk through topics at your level.

Who do I get matched with?

You're matched with other learners at your CEFR level (A1-C2) who share your topic interests, so the conversation stays natural and manageable.

What if I'm shy or run out of things to say?

Every room has ready-made question and topic prompts so you never freeze up. Starting with a familiar topic and someone at your level helps you feel more confident.

Does online speaking practice help with IELTS/TOEIC Speaking?

Yes. There are dedicated IELTS Speaking and TOEIC Speaking topic rooms so you can practice the exact question formats and answering reflexes.

How many times a week should I practice speaking?

At least 3-4 sessions a week, 15-20 minutes each. Speaking consistently matters more than one long session followed by a long break.

What do I need to start practicing?

Just a phone or computer with a mic and an internet connection. Practicing shadowing first helps you speak with more confident pronunciation.
