About the “Essential Words for the TOEFL” deck

Essential Words for the TOEFL is a set of 529 English words studied with spaced-repetition flashcards. Every card carries the word, its phonetic transcription, the meaning in your language and an example sentence, and it comes back for review right when you are about to forget it - so the words settle into long-term memory instead of fading after a day. The deck is aimed at learners around level A1.
